AI assistant FAQs

    

      
Who can use the AI assistant?
Admins, editors and viewers. LinearB scopes every answer to the teams that person can already see, so a team lead asking about delivery gets answers about their own teams.

      
Where do the answers come from?
From the same widgets and metric definitions behind your LinearB dashboards. Each answer names the widget, dashboard and date range it used, so you can open the same view and compare.

      
Can I keep an answer?
Answers stay in the chat, and you can copy them. To keep a metric on a dashboard, switch to Builder, describe the widget, and save it to Studio.

      
Can I see and edit the query Builder writes?
Yes. Builder shows the SQL behind every widget. You can edit it, run it again, and change the widget type and axes before you save.

      
Does it respect individual metric privacy settings?
Yes. If your organization has turned off individual-level metrics, the assistant doesn't return individual-level data.

      
How accurate are the answers?
LinearB grounds every answer in your LinearB data and names the widget it ran. As with any AI-generated answer, check important numbers against the dashboard before you act on them.
