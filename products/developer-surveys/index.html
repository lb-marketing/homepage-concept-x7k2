Developer surveys FAQs

We already run an engagement survey. Why another one?

Company engagement surveys cover everyone from sales to legal, use questions you didn’t write, and report by department weeks later. LinearB Surveys asks questions only engineers can answer, scores them by delivery team, in the same platform as the delivery metrics for those teams.

Are responses anonymous?

Yes. LinearB hides scores for any team, topic or question with fewer than 3 responses, and hides comments, open-text answers and multiple-choice selections with fewer than 5.

Who can see the results?

Primary Admins and Super Users. They create, launch and end surveys and view results. Team managers and other users don’t see survey results.

How do developers receive the survey?

Through a Slack direct message with a personal link, so they can answer without logging in to LinearB. Organizations without Slack can contact LinearB support about enabling Surveys. Read the Surveys docs →

Can I write my own questions?

Yes. Add your own topics and questions alongside the standard ones, as 1 to 5 ratings, multiple choice or open text. Ratings feed the scores. Multiple-choice answers show as their own results, and open-text answers come out in the CSV export.

How often should we run a survey?

We recommend an organization-wide survey each quarter, with pulse surveys for single teams in between. Duplicate a survey to run it again, so each round compares like with like.
